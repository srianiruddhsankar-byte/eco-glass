Test conditions

what does this control?
0 m ≈ surface, 6000 m ≈ near the deepest transoceanic cable routes. This sets hydrostatic pressure, which combines with bend cycles into the mechanical stress checked by rule F5. It's the same 0–6000 m scale used in the Cable lab, so results are comparable across both.
what does this control?
Deep ocean water sits close to 4°C year-round; this slider lets you test conditions from near-freezing to warm surface water. The signal-loss estimate treats 4°C as the reference point — the further you move from it, the more estimated loss it adds. This is this simulator's own simplified proxy, not a measured thermo-optic result.
what does this control?
0% = laid straight with no repeated flexing. 100% = the top of the load range this simulator models for handling, laying, and current-driven movement over time — not a real cycle count in millions of bends. It drives both the macrobend estimate (rule F2) and adds to the combined mechanical stress (rule F5).
